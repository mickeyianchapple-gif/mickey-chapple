Why temperament, and not just numbers

I spent fourteen years building toward one outcome, lost it in a single afternoon, and spent the months afterward working out what to do with what I had left. I am not going to claim that taught me more than a career does. But it did teach me something about my own reaction to a bad number, and I would rather have learned it then than on someone else's account.

It is also why the client side of this industry interests me more than the parts further from the people whose money it is.
