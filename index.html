Why temperament, and not just numbers

Technical skill is table stakes in this industry and it is teachable. Composure under a real loss is neither. I spent four years building toward one outcome, lost it in a single afternoon, and spent the months afterward deciding what to do with the capital I had left.

That is the same decision a client makes in a bad year, and it is the reason I chose wealth management rather than a desk further from the people whose money it is.
